BitCraft

3000×3000 world, deep caves, zombies, skeletons and rare Blasters that drop totems. If you die, the world is deleted. Save from the pause menu (☰ on touch, Esc on PC).

PC: WASD · Space jump/swim · Shift sprint · Mouse look · Left click break/attack · Right click place/eat/furnace · 1-9/wheel · E inventory
Touch: joystick, drag right side to look, L = break/attack, R = place/eat/use
