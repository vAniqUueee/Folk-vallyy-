Voxel Valley

An endless block world you can dig up and build on. Terrain, caves, trees, lakes and a day and night cycle are generated as you explore, and your edits are saved in this browser.

WASD
Move
Space
Jump, swim up. Double-tap to fly
Shift
Sprint, or fly down
Mouse
Look. Hold left to dig, right to place
1-9
Pick a slot (wheel works too)
Q
Copy the block you look at
F
Toggle flying
Esc
Pause. F3 shows or hides stats

WebGL is not available in this browser, so the 3D world cannot start.
