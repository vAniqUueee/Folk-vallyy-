⛏ Blockcraft

Huge 1000×1000 world with caves and lakes. Punch trees, craft on the 3×3 grid, mine ores, smelt them in a furnace. Hunt pigs for food; zombies roam at night. Day = 20 min, night = 10 min.

PC: WASD move · Space jump/swim · Shift sprint · Mouse look · Left click break/attack · Right click place / eat / use furnace · 1-9 or wheel · E inventory
Touch: joystick, drag right side to look, L = break/attack, R = place/eat/use
